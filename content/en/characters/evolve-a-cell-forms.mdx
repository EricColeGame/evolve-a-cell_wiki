## The core loop, as advertised

Read the official bullet points in order and a loop emerges. You start small and vulnerable. Plants are the low-risk food source; other cells, including players, are the high-reward one. Eating feeds growth, growth unlocks evolution, evolution adds the parts that make hunting and survival easier, and enough growth eventually puts you in front of bosses.

The PvP angle is the part worth internalizing early. Because other players are valid prey and valid predators, the pond is not a solo progression treadmill. A cell that only farms plants is competing on a slower curve than a cell that hunts, and it is also a target. The official page does not explain how death, respawn, or progression loss works, so the safest assumption until you check in-game is that survival matters.

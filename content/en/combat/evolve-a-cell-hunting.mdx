## Reading the Pond Before You Move

The most useful skill in a game like this is not a secret build order. It is threat assessment. The official description tells you the pond is full of hungry things, and that other cells include other players. That means every direction you swim has a possible cost.

Start by watching, not eating. Note which shapes are moving toward you, which are grazing, and which are ignoring everything. Because servers hold up to 16 players, the population is small enough that you can often learn individual players' habits within a session. A cell that chased you twice is likely to chase you a third time.

Plants are the advertised low-risk food source. Hunting is the advertised high-reward one. The official page does not state how much either is worth, and this article will not invent numbers. What you can verify in-game is which food keeps you alive longer relative to the risk you took to get it. That ratio is the real currency.

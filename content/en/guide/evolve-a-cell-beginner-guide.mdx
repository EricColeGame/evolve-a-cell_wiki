## Reading the pond: plants, players, and the food chain

The description splits your food into two categories: plants and other cells. That distinction shapes everything about how you play.

Plants are the low-risk option. They do not hunt you back, so eating them is the safest way to add mass while you are still small and fragile. The tradeoff is implied rather than documented: passive food is usually slower than hunting, but the official page does not state growth rates for either path, so I will not pretend to know exact numbers.

Hunting other cells is the high-risk, high-reward option, and it includes other players. With up to 16 players per server, you are rarely alone in the pond. That cuts both ways. More players means more potential prey, but it also means more things that can eat you before you finish growing. Early on, the sensible default is to treat every larger cell as a threat until you have a reason to think otherwise.

One practical habit: before you commit to hunting, watch how a target moves. Player-controlled cells tend to behave differently from passive ones, but the game does not document a targeting or lock-on system, so any specific hunting technique you read elsewhere is community trial and error, not confirmed mechanics.
